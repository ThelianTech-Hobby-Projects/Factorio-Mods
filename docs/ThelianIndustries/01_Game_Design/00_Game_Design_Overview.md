# Game Design Overview

**Status: Planning Draft**

## Purpose

This page reserves the canonical home for high-level Thelian Industries game-design summaries.

## Current source coverage

The migrated plans describe an overhaul direction with new mining, ore processing, smelting, intermediate products, construction materials, electronics, and an early interaction-oriented research progression. The owner-designated metallurgy plan locks its architecture through Decisions 1–8. The owner-designated [Electronics plan](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Electronics_Plan.md) locks a hybrid industrial abstraction with selective semiconductor depth, physically understandable early electronics, better same-output recipes where appropriate, distinct control and computing branches, and three progressively capable general-purpose production-machine tiers. These domain decisions still do not create a complete game-design specification or approved project-wide pillars.

Detailed progression is owned by [Game Progression](../02_Progression_and_Worlds/00_Game_Progression.md). Production details are owned by [Production Systems Overview](../03_Production_Systems/00_Production_Systems_Overview.md). Speculative concepts remain in the [Idea Backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md).
