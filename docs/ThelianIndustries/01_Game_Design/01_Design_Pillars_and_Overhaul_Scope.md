# Design Pillars and Overhaul Scope

**Status: Placeholder / Planning Required**

The current legacy Thelian Industries plans do not define approved design pillars or a formal overhaul scope. This document reserves the canonical location; no scope or pillar is inferred in this migration.

## Locked metallurgy principle

This is not a project-wide pillar set, but the metallurgy decision record locks one domain-specific principle: better technology should progressively increase the useful material and industrial value obtainable from finite geological resources. See [Thelian Industries Metallurgy Plan](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Metallurgy_Plan.md); it does not establish wider scope, release, or implementation commitments.

## Locked electronics principles

These are domain-specific constraints, not a project-wide pillar set. The [Thelian Industries Electronics Plan](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Electronics_Plan.md) locks:

- hybrid industrial abstraction with selective semiconductor depth;
- inventory granularity only where an item creates meaningful gameplay or manufacturing structure;
- progression through improved recipes/processes without automatic Mk-item proliferation;
- a functional distinction between machine control and general computing; and
- three general-purpose conventional electronics machine tiers rather than many narrowly process-specific buildings.

The plan leaves dependent-system details, implementation, art, and numerical balance deferred.
