# Victory and Postgame

**Status: Planning Draft**  
**Authority class: Canonical — resolved lifecycle terminology**  
**Sources:** [`Game-Progression-Tree.md`](../../../Mods/ThelianIndustries/plans/Game-Progression-Tree.md), [`Solar-System.md`](../../../Mods/ThelianIndustries/plans/Solar-System.md), and the non-canonical [`IDEAS.md`](../../../Mods/ThelianIndustries/plans/IDEAS.md)

## Resolved lifecycle

The project owner resolved the terminology during this migration:

1. Stage 5 contains the main victory path associated with reaching the **Solar System Edge**.
2. Reaching that milestone is the point at which the game is technically won.
3. **Stage 6** begins after that victory milestone.
4. **Archo Nexus** belongs to Stage 6 and is post-victory/postgame content.

This resolution does not define an objective, recipe, science cost, unlock prerequisite, or implementation behavior beyond the stated lifecycle.

## Stage 5 planning context

The legacy progression source places **Icarion's Grave** and the **Solar System Edge** in Stage 5, and its solar-system source labels the Solar System Edge as the “Win Condition Zone.” The owner resolution normalizes this into the main-victory milestone described above.

Icarion's Grave has an `AI Generated` world concept description in [Solar System and Planets](01_Solar_System_and_Planets.md). It is concept-draft material pending owner review, not approved lore or an implementation claim.

## Stage 6 and Archo Nexus

The legacy progression source places `Archo-Nexus` in Stage 6, and the solar-system source lists it under “Post Game Victory.” The active canonical display name is **Archo Nexus**. Its planet and orbit descriptions retain their `AI Generated` labels in [Solar System and Planets](01_Solar_System_and_Planets.md).

### Historical working name: The Ark

`The Ark` is not a separate current planet. The project owner resolved it as an early/generation-one working concept/name for the same final-world direction later named **Archo Nexus**.

The legacy idea backlog associated The Ark with a postgame bonus planet and a postgame science pack for infinite technology past level 10. Corresponding Archo-Nexus backlog entries describe a final postgame victory planet, Nanite Science, and research of infinite technologies past level 10. Those gameplay concepts remain **Idea / Candidate** material in the [Idea Backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md); this document records their historical routing and must not promote them to locked design.

## Current open specification gaps

The following are not defined by the migrated sources:

- the Stage 5 victory objective and exact path to Solar System Edge;
- Stage 6 unlocks, gates, research requirements, and implementation behavior;
- the exact behavior, recipe, cost, or research role of Nanite Science;
- the exact rules for infinite technologies past level 10.
