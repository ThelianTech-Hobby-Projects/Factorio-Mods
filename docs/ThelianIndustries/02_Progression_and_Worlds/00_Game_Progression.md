## Resolved lifecycle terminology

The project owner resolved the lifecycle relationship on 2026-09-04: **Stage 5 contains the main victory path associated with reaching the Solar System Edge. Stage 6 begins after that victory milestone and is post-victory/postgame progression. Archo Nexus belongs to Stage 6.** This is an intentional lifecycle, not an unresolved conflict.

## Stage 1 — Nauvis, Nauvis Orbit, and Luna

### Starting equipment and supplies

The source states that the player starts with starting gear and emergency supplies, using new battery-powered equipment, starter solar gear for the initial power network, and a battery charger.

The listed starting gear is:

- 3x Re-Charge Mining-Drills
- 6x Re-Charge Inserters
- 20x LumaCell Batteries
- 1x Battery Charger
- 1x Solar Panel
- 1x Pair of Jumper Cables
- 1x Re-Charge Ore Scanner
- 1x Re-Ore Crusher
- 1x Re-Charge Ore Sorter

The source includes the note: “Check if possible for some preferred mechanics.” No feasibility or implementation conclusion is made here.

### Starting resources and patches

**Status: Locked Concept Direction — Final Balance Values Not Yet Implemented**

The intended Stage 1 starting patches are visible, small, and of regular richness, with an approximate total yield of **25k–100k per starting patch**. This is the narrower starting-patch conceptual range; it must not be merged with the separate broader general-surface range documented by metallurgy.

The source lists these starting resources:

- Bituminous Ore
- Cuprite Ore
- Cassiterite Ore
- Galena Ore
- Hematite Ore
- Wood from trees
- Rubber from rubber trees
- Clay from dirt mounds

`Cassiterite` is the owner-approved active spelling; the source used `Casserite`.

### Stage goals

- Rocket Silo and Space Platform.
- Nauvis Orbit and Luna.
- Prepare for Stage 2.

### Nauvis ages and production direction

The source says Nauvis is changed and overhauled to have a Bronze Age, Iron Age, and Early Electronics Age.

- Copper and tin are the main metals used in the Bronze Age.
- Iron is the main metal used in the Iron Age.
- The Early Electronics Age begins electronics use for the rest of the game.

For that early electronics direction, the source names a new electronics chain using copper, tin, lead, and wood/stone to create electronics components. It lists resistors, capacitors, transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, and circuit boards, plus an Electronics Assembler. The electronics-use/progression concern is owned by the electronics documentation; this is a Stage 1 cross-reference only.

### Crafting and construction direction

The source identifies the following planning direction:

- New mining chains and mining machinery.
- New ore-processing and smelting chains.
- New intermediate products, including parts/components, byproducts and waste products, and metals/alloys.
- Buildings are “built” through a new crafting chain and new construction materials.
- Different buildings have different construction-part requirements, including Mechanical Parts, Hydraulic Parts, and Construction Parts (bundles of different parts).

Material production and exact construction-component definitions are not owned by this document. No recipes or requirements are inferred.

### Research progression direction

The source says the research chain is more interaction-based than science-pack-based, while still using science packs for certain things.

- Most early technologies progress through necessary crafting requirements.
- After the research desk is unlocked, different science data packets and other research supplies can be used to “theorize” new and advanced technologies, including infinite technology.

The exact technology tree, desk-unlock condition, data-packet system, and infinite-technology rules remain unspecified. See [Victory and Postgame](03_Victory_and_Postgame.md) for the separate postgame candidate concepts and the future research-design owner.
