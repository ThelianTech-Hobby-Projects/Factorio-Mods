# Research Index

**Status: Research Needed**

## Purpose

This index tracks evidence-gathering needs separately from design approval. A research result may inform an owner decision, but it does not itself promote an idea from the [Idea Backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md) into canonical design.

## Current research register

| Area | Status | Basis | Next decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical feasibility of selected speculative mechanics | Research Needed | Supplied planning audit, Section 10 | Validate with targeted prototypes before any implementation commitment. |
| Gameplay balance | Research Needed | Legacy plans lack complete recipes, values, and balance criteria | Owner must establish the intended balance scope and evidence requirements. |
| Real-world process reference | Placeholder / Research Needed | No dedicated legacy source | Owner must identify which processes, if any, need research and how it informs design. |

## Authority boundary

The [Idea Backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md) owns speculative concepts. The audit baseline is evidence context rather than gameplay design. See [Document Authority](../DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY.md) for the distinction between planned design and verified implementation.
