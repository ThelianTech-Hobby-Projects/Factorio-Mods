# Gameplay Balance Research

**Status: Research Needed**

## Current status

The legacy planning sources do not provide complete recipe tables, balance targets, craft times, machine speeds, technology costs, or a balance-validation methodology. This migration does not infer missing values or define balance objectives.

Where a source contains a planning range or yield relationship, its canonical owner remains the relevant progression or production-system document. This research page does not duplicate those values.

## Research questions

- What owner-approved balance goals and player-experience criteria should be used?
- Which source values are conceptual directions, and which require later tuning?
- What playtest and data evidence is required before a proposed balance change is accepted?
