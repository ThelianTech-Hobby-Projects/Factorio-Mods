# Plans and Backlog

**Status: Active planning backlog**  
**Authority class: Non-canonical — ideas, candidates, and research prompts**

This folder owns speculative Thelian Industries planning material. An entry here is not an approved design, implementation commitment, or source of truth for current mod behavior.

**Owner-designated sources:** [Thelian Industries Metallurgy Plan](InProgress%20Plans/TI_Metallurgy_Plan.md) is the source of truth for locked metallurgy architecture through Decisions 1–8. [Thelian Industries Electronics Plan](InProgress%20Plans/TI_Electronics_Plan.md) is the source of truth for the completed electronics architecture pass, with cross-system, Quantum/endgame, implementation, art, and balance details deferred. [Thelian Industries Research System Foundation Plan](InProgress%20Plans/TI_Research_System_Foundation_Plan.md) is the source of truth for the locked research architecture and rules; the complete technology tree remains deferred. These plans are not evidence of implementation. The Idea Backlog remains non-canonical.

The [Design Foundation and Overhaul Principles](../01_Game_Design/01_Design_Foundation_and_Overhaul_Principles.md) is the canonical project-wide design-constraint source. It guides cross-system reasoning but does not replace any owner-designated subsystem or Research/Game Progression authority.

The [TI Planning Overview](InProgress%20Plans/TI_Planning_Overview.md) is a high-level working inventory of current broad direction, future system and location planning, placeholder needs, and intentionally deferred work. It is not a locked design record and does not replace any detailed owner plan; its contents may change as future concept sessions refine the project.

Substantive future subsystem concept planning should apply the project-wide [Design Foundation and Overhaul Principles](../01_Game_Design/01_Design_Foundation_and_Overhaul_Principles.md), retain existing subsystem authority, and consult the latest [Idea Backlog](Idea_Backlog.md) for potentially relevant candidates without assuming they are approved.
