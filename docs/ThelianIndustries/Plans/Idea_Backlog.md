53. **Research Needed:** Explore control scripting for mechanics such as dynamically changing mining speeds and adding mining hardness, allowing mining speed and hardness gating behind technology and game stage.
54. **Candidate mechanics:** Change modules so each level is a final product; recycle modules to recover components for higher module tiers after the recipes unlock.


55. **Candidate mechanics / Research Needed:** Overhaul Nauvis pollution for the earliest progression tiers so primitive industry produces very little effective environmental pressure. Drastically increase natural pollution absorption on starting-planet terrain and vegetation, including dirt, sand, grass/forested terrain, and trees, with the design goal that these natural sinks can absorb far more pollution than vanilla values, potentially reaching hundreds of pollution units over the relevant game-time interval once balanced. At the same time, reduce pollution generation from Tier 0 / primitive processes such as contained campfires, wood burning, stone-brick furnaces, and similar early burner industry so a small settlement produces little or no meaningful pollution spread. Pollution should only begin spreading appreciably once the player builds a sufficiently large and industrialized operation. Research the actual Factorio 2.x pollution units, tile and tree absorption prototype properties, vanilla values, runtime behavior, and modding/API limits before assigning final numbers.
56. **Candidate mechanics / Research Needed:** Rebalance Nauvis enemy evolution and biter expansion to match the slower early pollution curve and the mod's extended progression. Review and tune how quickly evolution increases, when meaningful evolutionary pressure begins, how pollution/time/spawner destruction contribute, how soon biter expansion starts, how frequently new bases are founded, and other expansion parameters controlling settlement growth and pressure on the player. The intended result is lower and later enemy escalation during primitive/early industry, followed by progressively stronger expansion and evolution as industrial scale increases. Research which Factorio 2.x map settings, prototype values, per-surface settings, and control-stage scripting hooks can be used to implement planet-specific evolution and expansion behavior before locking exact values.


57. **Mandatory Research / Implementation Requirement:** Replace Nauvis starting-area resource generation with Thelian Industries starter resources instead of relying on the vanilla guaranteed starting set of iron, copper, coal, and stone. The current proposed starter set is **Copper, Tin, Lead, Coal, and Limestone**, with Limestone serving as the mod's stone-equivalent starting mineral. This needs explicit investigation and implementation planning for Factorio 2.x world generation: determine how Nauvis starting-area resource autoplace is defined, how vanilla starting-resource guarantees and starting-area placement interact with custom resource prototypes, how to remove or suppress unwanted vanilla starting ores, how to guarantee the TI starter resources spawn accessibly near the player, and how this should interact with broader resource-generation and planetary progression systems. The exact resource composition is a current proposed direction, but investigating and implementing TI-controlled Nauvis starting resources is mandatory.
