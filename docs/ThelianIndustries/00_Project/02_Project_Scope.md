# Project Scope

**Status: Planning Draft**

## Source-Backed In-Scope Planning Domains

The legacy planning set includes material or an explicitly reserved source for:

- game progression and stage sequencing;
- solar-system and planet concepts;
- metallurgy, mining, ore processing, ingots, alloys, parts, and byproducts;
- electronics and construction-component concepts;
- chemistry and oil processing;
- recycling and waste;
- recipe tables; and
- research and technology design.

The last four domains in the list have empty or very limited dedicated source material. Their destination documents must remain placeholders or narrowly source-backed planning notes until the owner supplies further design.

## Planning-Only Boundary

This migration does not establish source-code architecture, compatibility, tests, runtime behavior, final balance, technical feasibility, release plans, or implementation completion. Those claims require the evidence hierarchy in [Document Authority](../DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY.md).

## Backlog Boundary

The candidate mechanics in the idea backlog are not approved system scope. The [idea backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md) remains their authoritative owner unless the project owner explicitly promotes an item.
