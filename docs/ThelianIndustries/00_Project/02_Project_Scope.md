# Project Scope

**Status: Planning Draft**

## Source-Backed In-Scope Planning Domains

The legacy planning set includes material or an explicitly reserved source for:

- game progression and stage sequencing;
- solar-system and planet concepts;
- metallurgy, mining, ore processing, ingots, alloys, parts, and byproducts;
- locked electronics architecture/interfaces and construction-component concepts;
- chemistry and oil processing;
- recycling and waste;
- recipe tables; and
- research and technology design.

Chemistry/oil processing, recycling/waste, and numeric recipe tables still lack complete dedicated specifications. The research-system foundation is now locked in the [Research System Foundation Plan](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Research_System_Foundation_Plan.md), but the unified detailed technology tree and progression graph remain future planning work. The Electronics architecture and research foundation do not finalize dependent-system chains, exact balance, or implementation.

The [TI Planning Overview](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Planning_Overview.md) is the current high-level inventory for broader future system and location planning, including work not represented by a dedicated owner plan yet. Its summaries are working direction and planning coordination, not new scope approvals or a replacement for this document's detailed owners; future concept-planning sessions should consult the latest [Idea Backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md) for topic-relevant candidates without assuming they are approved.

## Planning-Only Boundary

This migration does not establish source-code architecture, compatibility, tests, runtime behavior, final balance, technical feasibility, release plans, or implementation completion. Those claims require the evidence hierarchy in [Document Authority](../DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY.md).

## Backlog Boundary

The candidate mechanics in the idea backlog are not approved system scope. The [idea backlog](../Plans/Idea_Backlog.md) remains their authoritative owner unless the project owner explicitly promotes an item.
