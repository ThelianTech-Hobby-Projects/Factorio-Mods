## Current Proposed Direction — Exact Resource Allocation Not Final

The planetary metallurgy progression and the use of additional planet-specific metals are part of the current planning direction. The exact assignment of Titanium, Cobalt/Cobaltite, and other additional metals between Tectara, Vulcanus, and later planets has not yet been finalized for implementation.

Current source statements associate Tectara with Titanium and Cobalt in one planning context, while the metallurgy source lists Titanium on Tectara and Cobaltite on Vulcanus. Preserve both statements; do not select an allocation without a newer owner decision.
