# Current Project Plan

**Status: Planning Draft**

The legacy progression source provides the following game-stage structure. It is a planning sequence, not a verified implementation or completed roadmap.

| Stage | Current source-backed scope |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Nauvis, Nauvis Orbit, and Luna; starting gear, resources, ages, construction, and early research direction are described. |
| 2 | Vulcanus, Gleba, and Fulgora are named; the source leaves the stage detail empty. |
| 3 | Voltaris, Pyrosauria, and Tectara are named; the source leaves the stage detail empty. |
| 4 | Aquilo and Zephyrus are named; owner resolution identifies Aquilo as the vanilla Space Age planet and says mod-specific changes are not yet specified. |
| 5 | Icarion's Grave and the Solar System Edge are named. The Solar System Edge is the main victory milestone under the owner resolution. |
| 6 | Archo Nexus is named. Under the owner resolution, it is post-victory/postgame progression after Stage 5. |

The detailed progression owner is [Progression and Worlds](../02_Progression_and_Worlds/README.md). The Solar System Edge, Archo Nexus, and postgame relationship are governed by its victory/postgame document.
