# Trains and Ground Transportation

**Status:** Placeholder / Planning Required

## Purpose

This document reserves the planning location for a future Trains and Ground Transportation Improvement Plan. Vanilla rail-network behavior remains the baseline; any TI additions should be considered without presuming that the core train network needs replacement.

## Current planning direction

The [TI Planning Overview](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Planning_Overview.md) identifies this as future planning work. Locomotive or transport-class extensions may be investigated, with diesel locomotives, fluid-fueled locomotive concepts, steam-locomotive concepts, and related transport extensions as examples under consideration. These are not approved mechanics. Technical feasibility—including any direct-fluid-fuel concept—remains for future research and planning; this page does not claim that Factorio currently supports it.
