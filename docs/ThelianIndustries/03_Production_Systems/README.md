# Production Systems

**Status: Planning Draft**

This domain owns material processing, yield rules, and other production-system planning. Electronics now has a locked architecture summarized here and detailed in its current plan. Chemistry and Recipe Tables remain incomplete general domains but record narrow locked cross-system interfaces; other source domains remain explicit placeholders where stated.

The [TI Planning Overview](../Plans/InProgress%20Plans/TI_Planning_Overview.md) inventories the expected future planning work for production machines, chemistry and oil, recycling, construction components, fuel/energy, and recipe/balance frameworks. It does not supply those systems' missing specifications or supersede their owner pages.
